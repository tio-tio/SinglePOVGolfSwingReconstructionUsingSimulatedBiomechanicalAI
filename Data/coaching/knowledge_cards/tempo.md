# Swing Tempo

## Metadata

- **Indicator key:** `tempo_ratio`
- **Label:** Tempo
- **Plain-language name:** The timing relationship between the backswing and downswing
- **Swing phase:** Overall swing
- **Unit:** Ratio

## Measurement definition

Measures how long the backswing took compared with the downswing.

A ratio near `3:1` means the backswing lasted about three times as long as the downswing.

## Beginner explanation

Tempo describes the timing and rhythm of the swing. It compares the duration of the backswing with the duration of the downswing; it does not directly measure how fast the club or body moved.

## Why it matters

This measurement helps describe the timing pattern of the swing. It does not by itself determine smoothness, consistency, power, contact quality, or whether the golfer should swing faster or slower.

## Evidence classification

- **Directly measured:** The ratio of backswing duration to downswing duration.
- **Derived comparison:** Whether the ratio was inside, below, or above the tour reference range.
- **Not supported:** Clubhead speed, hand speed, power, smoothness, rhythm quality, transition quality, contact quality, ball flight, or whether the golfer should speed up or slow down.

## Approved interpretation language

### Inside the reference range

The measured backswing-to-downswing timing ratio was within the tour reference range.

### Below the reference range

The measured tempo ratio was below the tour reference range, meaning the backswing was shorter relative to the downswing than in the comparison range.

### Above the reference range

The measured tempo ratio was above the tour reference range, meaning the backswing was longer relative to the downswing than in the comparison range.

## Approved glossary

- **Tempo:** The timing relationship between the backswing and downswing.
- **Tempo ratio:** The backswing duration divided by the downswing duration.
- **Three-to-one ratio:** A backswing that takes about three times as long as the downswing.
- **Backswing:** The part of the swing moving away from the target before the club changes direction.
- **Downswing:** The part of the swing moving from the top toward impact.
- **Tour reference range:** The typical spread of measured values seen across the professional swings used for comparison.

## Comparison language

- The measured tempo ratio increased compared with the earlier swing.
- The measured tempo ratio decreased compared with the earlier swing.
- The measured tempo ratio was similar in both swings.
- The backswing became longer relative to the downswing.
- The backswing became shorter relative to the downswing.
- The current measurement moved into the tour reference range.
- The current measurement moved outside the tour reference range.
- The current and earlier measurements were both inside the tour reference range.
- The current and earlier measurements were both outside the tour reference range.

## Confidence and camera limitations

- This measurement depends on accurate detection of the beginning of the swing, the top of the backswing, and impact.
- Errors in event detection or missing video frames can affect the calculated ratio.
- It should not be interpreted or compared when the scorecard marks the measurement as low confidence.
- Tempo is a timing ratio and should not be described as clubhead speed, hand speed, or overall swing speed.
- Two swings can have a similar tempo ratio while taking different total amounts of time.
- The tour range is a comparison reference, not an ideal target for every golfer.

## Allowed inferences

The LLM may:

- Describe the measured backswing-to-downswing timing relationship.
- State whether the ratio was inside, below, or above the tour reference range.
- Explain what a three-to-one ratio means.
- Compare reliable tempo ratios between two swings.
- State whether the backswing became longer or shorter relative to the downswing.
- Describe the timing pattern neutrally.
- State when the measurement is not reliable enough to assess.

## Prohibited inferences

The LLM must not:

- Say the golfer swung too fast or too slowly based only on the tempo ratio.
- Describe the tempo as correct, incorrect, good, bad, perfect, smooth, rushed, or faulty solely from the reference comparison.
- Treat a three-to-one ratio as a required or universally ideal target.
- Claim that the ratio created or reduced power, distance, consistency, or contact quality.
- Infer clubhead speed, hand speed, acceleration, transition force, or total swing duration from the ratio alone.
- Claim that a lower ratio necessarily means the golfer rushed the downswing.
- Claim that a higher ratio necessarily means the golfer had a slow swing.
- Predict ball direction, trajectory, carry, slice, hook, fade, or draw.
- Recommend changing backswing speed, downswing speed, rhythm, or timing.
- Interpret or reveal a value marked as low confidence.

## Approved response examples

### Inside the reference range

Your measured backswing-to-downswing timing ratio was within the tour reference range. This means the timing relationship between the two parts of the swing fell within the comparison range.

### Below the reference range

Your measured tempo ratio was below the tour reference range. In this swing, the backswing took less time relative to the downswing than in the comparison range.

### Above the reference range

Your measured tempo ratio was above the tour reference range. In this swing, the backswing took more time relative to the downswing than in the comparison range.

### Explaining the ratio

A three-to-one tempo ratio means the backswing lasts about three times as long as the downswing. It describes relative timing, not how fast the club was travelling.

### Comparison — increased

Your measured tempo ratio increased compared with the earlier swing. That means the backswing became longer relative to the downswing.

### Comparison — decreased

Your measured tempo ratio decreased compared with the earlier swing. That means the backswing became shorter relative to the downswing.

### Low confidence

The swing events were not detected reliably enough to assess the tempo ratio confidently for this swing.
